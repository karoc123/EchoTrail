# Prague in the Morning Light

I got up just after sunrise and walked to the Old Town Square —
and had the fortune of having it almost entirely to myself.

The cobblestones still glistened from the overnight rain.
The astronomical clock struck seven, and a few pigeons took flight.
No tourist crowds, no tour groups. Just me, the square and the silence.

## The Golden City

Prague is incredibly dense. Every alley holds history,
at every corner waits a café whose interior seems
to have remained unchanged for decades.

I got lost — deliberately, after the third coffee.
Somewhere behind St. Vitus Cathedral, a narrow path runs along the castle wall
that is not even marked on Google Maps.

**Recommendation**: Come early, put away the map, just walk.

## Dinner

Svíčková by the Vltava. Eaten in silence, with the best view of the day.
