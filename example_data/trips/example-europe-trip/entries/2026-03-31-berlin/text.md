# Departure from Berlin

Early in the morning at half past six — the alarm rips me from a deep sleep.
The backpack has been packed since last night, the motorcycle stands ready at the door.

One last coffee, then off we go. The streets of Berlin are still quiet,
only a few cars and a fresh breeze greet me.
The city motorway leads me south, and the further I ride,
the stronger the butterflies in my stomach.

## First Impressions

Spring has not quite arrived yet — bare trees line the country road,
and the fields still lie in a rich grey-brown.
But somewhere on the horizon, just past Luckenwalde,
the sun breaks through the clouds for the first time. A good sign.

Lunch break at a small rest stop on the B96: a sandwich,
a thermos of coffee, and the first page of my travel diary.

_Day 1 of 21. It begins._
